    Rules of Leduc hold'em

    Six cards: two each of J, Q and K. Both players ante 1 and get one private card. In round one bets and raises are 2. Then one board card is dealt and round two bets and raises are 4. Each round allows a bet plus one raise. At showdown, matching the board makes a pair and wins. Otherwise the higher card wins and equal cards split the pot. Seats swap every hand and seat 1 acts first in both rounds.
